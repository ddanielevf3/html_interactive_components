Scratch card & gamification

very limited support
Interactive module · reference sheet

A "scratch and win" or playful reveal effect for uncovering a discount code. One of the most visually striking modules and, along with video, one of the least supported: realistically reliable only in Apple Mail.

When to propose it: campaigns aimed at strongly Apple-leaning user bases (fashion, premium lifestyle), always with the prize already visible in the fallback.

Fallback: the prize/code must be shown already uncovered and legible in the static version — never hidden behind an interaction that, in many clients, can never happen.

Additional notes

Asset and flow requirements, taken from the internal component library.

  • 1 GIF (no retina format required).
  • 1 “Scratch Me” image in retina (x2) format.
  • The “scratch and reveal” happens on the landing page, not inside the email: clicking the cover image in the email leads to the landing page, where the recipient can scratch it to reveal the prize and the CTA button underneath.
