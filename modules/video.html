MP4 Video

limited support
Interactive module · reference sheet

A video player embedded directly in the email with the web's <video> tag. Playable only in Apple Mail; everywhere else the video simply doesn't appear unless an explicit fallback is provided. Its impact on metrics is among the highest in the whole catalog: opens +6%, click-through +65%, unsubscribes -26% — and yet only 34% of marketers actually use it, even though 50% recognise its value.

Technical requirements: the video file must be hosted on your own server or on a sending platform (CRM/ESP) — it isn't possible to embed a YouTube link or ID, you need a direct URL to the file (e.g. https://yoursite.com/video/filename.mp4). Keep it lightweight: if embedded, under 10MB, 30-60 seconds, 600px wide.

Fallback: a "poster" image — a still frame from the video with a play icon overlaid, optionally with a short instruction — under 100-200 KB, clickable through to the landing page where the video is actually hosted. Avoid a GIF as the fallback: only a few clients support it.

When to propose it: never as the only content — always paired with a clickable preview that opens the video on the landing page.

Additional notes

Asset requirements, taken from the internal component library.

  • Direct link to the hosted video file — no YouTube or Vimeo.
  • Video length: 30–60 seconds.
  • 1 poster image (with or without a play icon) in retina (x2) format.
  • 1 fallback image in retina (x2) format.
