Interactive Hotspot

mixed support
Interactive module · reference sheet

Clickable areas layered on top of an image (e.g. points on a photo of an outfit that reveal the price of each item), which the recipient discovers on mouse hover or tap. They organise content, highlight key products and allow more calls to action without visually crowding the email — and they generate engagement data that's useful for optimising future campaigns.

Reference figure: around 79% of email clients support the fully interactive version of hotspots (internal source on client market share). On a base analysed by Litmus with Gmail (36.8%), Apple Mail (20%) and iPhone (13.2%) — 70% of the total — only 30% of subscribers would see the fallback. Always check the client distribution of the specific list for that market/segment, not the industry average.

Fallback: a single static image summarising all the hotspots (optionally a different version for mobile); avoid a GIF as the fallback.

When to propose it: lookbooks, catalogues with several products in a single lifestyle image.
